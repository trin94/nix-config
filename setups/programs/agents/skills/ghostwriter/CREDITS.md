## plain-english

The [Cuts](SKILL.md#cuts) and [Tics](SKILL.md#tics) rules, and most of
[`RULES.md`](RULES.md), are adapted from the **plain-english** skill by
**b1rdmania**:

<https://github.com/b1rdmania/claude-plain-english-skill>

That skill declares MIT in its README. It ships no `LICENSE` file and no
copyright line, so none is reproduced here.

What ghostwriter changed: the two passes were merged into one skill and
condensed, the voice and accent sections were added, and the tables were
reworded to drop em-dashes. The substitution lists, the false-limb table, the
banned openers and closers, and the before/after examples are substantially
that author's work.

## The tradition behind it

- George Orwell, *Politics and the English Language* (1946)
- Sir Ernest Gowers, *Plain Words* (1948)
- Sir Ernest Gowers, *The ABC of Plain Words* (1951)
