## domain-modeling

[`SKILL.md`](SKILL.md), [`ADR-FORMAT.md`](ADR-FORMAT.md),
[`GLOSSARY-FORMAT.md`](GLOSSARY-FORMAT.md) and `agents/` are copied from the
**domain-modeling** skill by **Matt Pocock**:

<https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/tree/v1.3.1/skills/engineering/domain-modeling>

What this copy changed in [`ADR-FORMAT.md`](ADR-FORMAT.md): a "Keep it to the
essence" section keeps ADRs to the decision and leaves details to code, docs
and git history; a changed decision rewrites its ADR, so the `superseded by`
status was dropped.

What this copy changed in [`SKILL.md`](SKILL.md): ADRs are never written or
offered during the session; a decision that earns one becomes work in the first
ticket unless the user says otherwise.
